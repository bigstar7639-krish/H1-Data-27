Q+A Katha - Pazhamozhi 🧠

Question: What was Ash Ketchum's second Pokemon?

Answer: Caterpie - Entertainment: Japanese Anime & Manga. Itha kelvi Pazhamozhi pathi - Answer Caterpie. Oru naal oru chinna oor la oru paiyan irunthan, avan manasula Pazhamozhi pathi neraya yosichittu irunthan, yaar kitta sollurathu nu theriyama thaniya irunthan, appo avanukku oru nalla friend kedaichan, antha friend avan kooda pesi avan manasa purinjikittan, kadasiya avan Pazhamozhi la irunthu nalla purithaloda sirichuttu veetuku ponan, ithu thaan Pazhamozhi oda azhagu, kashtam vanthalum nambikkaiya iruntha ellam sari aayidum nu avan purinjikittan.

Question: Talos, the mythical giant bronze man, was the protector of which island?

Answer: Crete - Mythology. Itha kelvi Joke pathi - Answer Crete. Joke pathi kelvi patta oru ponnu, ava daily Joke pathi yosichittu iruntha, oru naal ava oru puthu book padichu Joke oda unmaiya purinjikita, athula irunthu ava life maariduchu, ava bayapadama Joke ah face panna aarambicha, ava friends ellam ava maariya paathu aachariyapattanga, kadasiya ava Joke la jeyichu santhosham ah iruntha, ithu thaan namakku kathai sollura paadam.
